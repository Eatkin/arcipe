Saved plans

Plan a week, press Save plan, and it shows up here. Tick meals off as you cook them and their ingredients come out of the pantry.
Week not going to plan?
Re-plan the days from today with what's in the pantry now. Meals you've ticked off, locked 🔒 or skipped stay as they are.

Shopping

Ticked things go straight into the pantry, with their usual shelf life from today.

What's in. Amount is a count (“3” carrots) or a weight (“500g”). Use-by is the last good day; leave it blank for things that keep. Changes save as you go. 🗑 means it went in the bin (counted as waste); × means it's used up.

Cooked, in the fridge

Leftovers from meals you've ticked off. The plan serves them before cooking something new.

Add lots at once

Everything the planner knows, A to Z. Type an amount next to what you've got (a count or grams, as above), then add them all. “in” means you've already some listed.

Assumed always in

Staples you nearly always have, so you don't list them: spices, oil, flour and so on. Untick any you've run out of or don't keep: plans will shop for them, or skip dishes that need them. (Adding one to the pantry above also tracks how much you have.)
