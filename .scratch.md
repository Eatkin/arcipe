IMPROVEMENTS (2026-11-25), roughly in priority order. Tiers 1-2 make it usable
week to week; 3-5 make the plans better. [new] = noticed this session.

== 1. The weekly loop: plan, shop, cook, adjust ==================================
Saving and ticking work now. Next is coping when the week doesn't go to plan.
  a. [new] Plan the rest of the week: on a saved plan, keep the ticked meals and
     re-plan the remaining days from today's pantry (after a takeaway, a missed
     shop, a gift of courgettes). Probably the most useful next step.
  b. [new] Edit the plan by hand: lock a meal ("chilli on Friday") and plan around
     it; swap a meal for one of the next-best options; skip a meal (eating out),
     which also changes how many people a pot needs to feed.
  c. [new] Cooked it differently: when ticking off, let the amounts be edited, or
     tick "made something else" and pick the recipe. At the moment it trusts the
     plan's grams.
  d. [new] Binning and real waste: a "binned" button on pantry rows and dishes
     that logs real waste. Show gone-off items with a nudge, and a running "wasted
     this month" figure (actual, not predicted).
  e. [new] Ratings on saved plans: 👍/👎 shows on saved meals but isn't stored on
     the plan, so it's forgotten on reload. Keep it on the meal, and prompt for a
     rating after ticking "Cooked".
  f. [new] Shopping list: copy as text or share to a phone, group by shop section,
     and allow a mid-week top-up shop (plans only shop on day 1 at the moment).
  g. [new] Untick after editing the pantry only puts back what it can (it matches
     lots by id and use-by). Fine for now; a proper pantry history/undo log would
     make it exact.
  h. Saved plans can't be renamed yet.
  i. [new] Ticking from the kitchen: the dashboard binds to 127.0.0.1, so not from
     a phone. An opt-in `--lan` flag would do it, but think about it first: there's
     no auth, so anyone on the wifi could edit the pantry.

== 4. Model features =============================================================
  a. Freezer: cooked beans and batch dishes into the freezer (long keeps_days, a
     defrost step). Pairs with 1c: "froze the rest" when ticking off.
  b. Opened tins/jars: half a tin of coconut cream keeps ~3 days once opened,
     unopened forever. Storage areas could follow (opened jars → fridge).
  c. Whole units: cutting a carrot is fine, half an avocado isn't; the scraps rule
     only covers the end state.
  d. A hands-on time limit might suit "low effort" nights better than total time.
  e. Vibes: an affinity prior from flavour notes (unused in scoring at the
     moment) alongside 👍/👎.
  f. Leftovers left in the fridge at the end of a plan are free unless they go off
     within SPILL_DAYS. Fine while plans re-plan weekly; revisit with 1a.
  g. [new] Pantry leftovers only get served if there's a portion for everyone
     (portions >= people). One portion of chilli + something else isn't an option.
